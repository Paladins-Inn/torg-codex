== Numbering

Existing records are numbered consecutively from 001 through 017. Numbers 018 and
019 are reserved for the pending multi-valued-reference and domain-level DRM
proposals respectively. Their feature plans create and register those records;
they are not accepted decisions or linked pages yet.

[cols="1,3,3",options="header"]

| Reserved number | Proposal | Feature plan
| 018 | Multi-valued references and encoded-list repair | `specs/004-multi-cosm-assignment/`
| 019 | Domain-level product-ownership DRM | `specs/005-domain-drm-enforcement/`
|===
